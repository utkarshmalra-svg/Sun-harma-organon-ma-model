# Sun Pharma–Organon M&A Financial Model

## Project Overview

This project presents an illustrative Mergers & Acquisitions (M&A) financial model for a potential acquisition of Organon & Co. by Sun Pharmaceutical Industries Ltd.

The model covers:

- Transaction assumptions
- Purchase price calculation
- Revenue synergies
- Transaction fee estimation
- Pro forma cost of debt
- Pro forma net income
- Pro forma EPS
- Accretion & dilution analysis

# Screenshots

## 1. Financial Inputs

- Acquirer (Sun Pharma): share price ₹1,620, 240 Cr shares outstanding, net income ₹11,509 Cr.
- Acquirer tax and debt cost: 25% tax rate and a 7.41% cost of debt.
- Target (Organon Co.): share price 1,068.574, 28.5 Cr shares outstanding, net income 1,638.12 Cr, tax rate 24.40%.

![Financial Inputs](01_Financial_Inputs.png)

---

## 2. Transaction Assumptions

- Purchase price: deal price of 1,328.6 per share × 28.5 Cr shares = 37,865.1 Cr total equity value.
- Financing mix: 100% cash consideration, with no stock or debt consideration.
- Other deal inputs: pre-tax synergies of 277.60 Cr and transaction fees of 549.04 Cr.

![Transaction Assumptions](02_Transaction_Assumptions.png)

## 2. Pro Forma Assumptions

- Pro forma net income: Sun Pharma 11,509 + Organon 1,638.12 + after-tax synergies 208.20, less after-tax interest on new financing, gives 12,613 Cr.
- After-tax synergies: 277.60 × (1 − 25%) = 208.20 Cr.
- Share count: no new shares are issued (all-cash deal), so pro forma shares stay at 240 Cr.

![Transaction Assumptions](03_Pro_Forma_Assumptions.png)

---

## 3. Revenue Synergies

- Synergy ramp-up: synergies as a % of target revenue rise from 0.5% (Years 0–1) to 1.0%, 1.5% and 2.0% by Year 4.
- Target revenue forecast: grows from 55,520 Cr (Year 0) to 61,523 Cr (Year 4), at 2.3% to 2.9% annual growth.
- Resulting synergies: 277.6 → 284.0 → 582.2 → 896.8 → 1,230.5 Cr by Year 4.

![Revenue Synergies](04_Revenue_Synergies.png)

---

## 4. Pro Forma Cost of Debt

- Existing debt: Sun Pharma 4,627 Cr at 8.16% (INR) and Organon 863.8 Cr at 4.87% (USD).
- New debt: 975 Cr issued by Sun Pharma at 6.15% (USD).
- Blended result: the weighted-average pro forma cost of debt is 7.41%, which feeds the interest assumption (5.56% after tax).

![Pro Forma Cost of Debt](05_Pro_Forma_Cost_of_Debt.png)

## 6. Accretion & Dilution Analysis

- EPS comparison: standalone EPS of 47.95 vs pro forma EPS of 52.55.
- Accretion: pro forma EPS is 9.59% higher, so the deal is accretive.
- Interpretation: synergies and financing assumptions outweigh acquisition-related costs, so the deal is expected to enhance shareholder earnings.
